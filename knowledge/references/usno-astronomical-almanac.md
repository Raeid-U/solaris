## Scope

This source describes the annual Astronomical Almanac and its contents. It is a reference publication for fundamental astronomical data.

## Extracted Takeaways

- The Almanac is a joint USNO/HMNAO publication.
- It includes sections on phenomena, time scales, coordinate systems, the Sun, the Moon, planets, tables, notes, references, and glossary material.
- Solar material includes Sun coordinates, physical ephemerides, times of transit, and equation of time.
- Lunar material includes phases, orbital/rotational elements, coordinates, librations, and related data.
- The Almanac is available one year in advance of its date.

## Formulas / Locators

- Section B: time scales and coordinate systems.
